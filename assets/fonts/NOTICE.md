JetBrainsMono-NFM-subset.ttf
============================

A subset of "JetBrainsMono Nerd Font Mono" v3.4.0 (Regular weight),
bundled by Octodo as its guaranteed monospace fallback font and icon
fallback. Upstream font is JetBrains Mono (SIL Open Font License 1.1 —
see OFL.txt); the Nerd Fonts patch (which already applies the OFL
rename to "JetBrainsMono NFM") merges icon glyphs from the projects
listed below. Regenerate with tool/make_font_subset.py.

Subsetting: Latin/Greek/Cyrillic text ranges plus the icon blocks
E000-E00F, E0A0-E0D4, E5FA-E62B, E700-E7C5, EA60-EBEB, ED00-ED5F,
F400-F4FC (see tool/make_font_subset.py for the exact list). The
supplementary-plane Material Design Icons are not included.
